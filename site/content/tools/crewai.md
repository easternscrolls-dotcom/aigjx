CrewAI is an open-source framework for orchestrating role-playing, autonomous AI
agents that collaborate as a "crew" to accomplish complex multi-step tasks.

## Highlights

- Define agents with roles, goals, and backstories.
- Tasks are assigned and handed off between agents.
- Lightweight and independent of heavier orchestration stacks.

Useful for modeling team-like division of labor among agents.
