Zapier is a no-code automation platform that connects thousands of apps through
"Zaps" — automated workflows triggered by events — including built-in AI steps.

## Highlights

- Massive app directory (thousands of integrations).
- No-code editor with conditional logic and multi-step Zaps.
- AI features for formatting, summarization, and routing.

Ideal for non-developers who want reliable, hosted automation out of the box.
