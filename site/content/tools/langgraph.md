LangGraph is a library for building stateful, multi-agent workflows as graphs,
offering fine-grained control over loops, branching, and human-in-the-loop
steps on top of LangChain.

## Highlights

- Explicit graph-based control flow (nodes, edges, conditional routing).
- Designed for long-running, stateful agents and multi-agent systems.
- Supports persistence, checkpointing, and interruption for human review.

A strong choice when you need deterministic, inspectable agent orchestration.
