**Agentic Tools** is an editorially-curated directory of AI agents, agent
frameworks, and automation platforms. The goal is a clean, fast, ad-light
reference you can scan to discover the right tool — without wading through
affiliate-heavy "best of" lists.

## How it's maintained

- Listings are written by hand and kept concise and factual.
- Each entry links out to the tool's official website for the latest pricing,
  features, and availability.
- The site is a static Hugo site deployed on Cloudflare Pages; content is plain
  Markdown, so it is easy to audit and update.

## A note on accuracy

AI tooling moves fast. Pricing tiers, feature sets, and even product names
change frequently. Treat every listing as a starting point and **verify the
details on the official site** before making decisions. Agentic Tools is not
affiliated with the products listed unless explicitly stated.
